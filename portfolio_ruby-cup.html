The situation

When I joined Ruby Cup as Marketing Manager in October 2023, the B2B side of the business was effectively non-existent. There were no enterprise sales materials, no B2B content strategy, and no external agency relationships managing the digital infrastructure. The website was underperforming, and there was no coordinated PR or out-of-home presence.

At the same time, Ruby Cup — a social enterprise operating on a buy-one-give-one model — had a genuinely compelling story for corporate partners. The challenge was building the machinery to tell it.

What I built

B2B marketing programme: I created the full B2B infrastructure from scratch — pitch decks, partnership sales collateral, case study content and a B2B landing page — positioning Ruby Cup as a credible corporate partner for workplace wellbeing, CSR and sustainability programmes. This directly secured the brand's first enterprise clients.

Website redevelopment: I led an end-to-end website overhaul, briefing and managing a cross-functional team of 10 collaborators including a copywriter, UX/UI expert, designer, ads specialists and the external web development agency. I wrote the briefs, managed timelines, reviewed deliverables, and ensured the new site reflected both the brand's values and its commercial goals.

"Managing a 10-person cross-functional team across a full website redevelopment — while simultaneously running the day-to-day marketing programme — required the same skills as agency management at a small marketing firm."

The result was a +92% improvement in conversion rate, achieved through a combination of cleaner user journeys, stronger on-page copy, and strategic A/B testing of landing page elements.

PR and media coverage: I managed relationships with multiple PR agencies, writing briefs and press releases that resulted in coverage in Cosmopolitan and other national media. I also liaised with Global for the brand's OOH advertising campaign, overseeing creative direction and copy.

CSR and impact reporting: As part of the B2B programme, I worked on Ruby Cup's CSR output — presenting investment proposals for social enterprise and charity partnerships to internal stakeholders, managing impact budgets, and producing content that communicated social return on investment to corporate audiences.

Skills demonstrated

  • Agency management: Briefing, managing and evaluating multiple external partners simultaneously
  • B2B copywriting: Pitch decks, sales collateral, partnership content for a corporate audience
  • Project management: Coordinating a 10-person team across a major digital delivery
  • PR writing: Press releases, media briefs, coverage strategy
  • Impact communication: Translating CSR data and social outcomes into compelling stakeholder content
  • Conversion optimisation: Funnel analysis, landing page redesign, A/B testing
