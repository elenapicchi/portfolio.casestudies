The context

Nomysh was a creator economy platform — helping content creators monetise through affiliate marketing, brand partnerships and UGC. When I joined in October 2022, there was no marketing infrastructure, no established brand voice, and no content strategy.

The audience was B2B in nature: creators who thought of themselves as micro-businesses, and brands looking to partner with them. The content had to work for both sides — educational enough to build creator trust, sophisticated enough to convert brand partners.

What I built

Brand and messaging from scratch. I developed Nomysh's brand identity, tone of voice and core messaging architecture through customer research and persona development — building a positioning that differentiated the platform in a crowded creator tools market.

Thought leadership content programme. I wrote and produced a library of long-form blog articles, webinars and video content covering the economics of content creation, brand pitching, affiliate marketing strategy and creator business models. I hosted webinars and appeared on camera in educational video content — producing material that established Nomysh as a credible voice in the creator economy space.

Writing about the economics of content creation for an audience of self-employed creators required exactly the same skill as writing about investment for founders — making financial concepts feel relevant, actionable and trustworthy.

B2B enterprise programme. I identified and secured Nomysh's first B2B enterprise clients, developing partnership propositions and sales materials that framed the platform's value for brand partners — not just creators.

CRM and lifecycle marketing. I built and managed the full CRM infrastructure — segmentation, automation, lifecycle flows — to convert acquired users into active platform participants and retain them over time.
