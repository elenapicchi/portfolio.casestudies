The brief

Every part of the influencer and content mix

Influencer marketing includes different audience-sized content creator: macro names for reach, micro creators for trust, and UGC for authenticity. On top of that, original in-house content keeps the audience engaged while having full control over the topics and production for the ongoing calendar. I've built and run all four, plus the creative direction.

Macro influencer partnerships

Across my career, I sourced and secured placements with creators reaching over 1 Million impressions across various platforms, including a shared-asset collaboration, shooting with the influencers directly, or having them simply sharing about us on their platforms.
