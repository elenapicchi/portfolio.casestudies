I built Ruby Cup's influencer and content programme from relationship-building through to paid media, and rebuilt the email programme alongside it — two channels that used to generate almost nothing, each independently growing to a third of monthly revenue.

The brief

Building relationships that actually convert

Influencer marketing is easy to do badly — free product for a story that disappears in 24 hours. I wanted a programme that built real, ongoing relationships with creators, and made sure their content had a life beyond the original post: as ads, as owned social content, as a genuine revenue channel.

Influencer partnerships

  • Sourced and reached out to creators across the spectrum — from small, highly engaged micro-creators to established names with six-figure followings
  • Built moodboards, negotiated and finalised deals and contracts, managed product sends and follow-up
  • Repurposed creator content as both organic social posts and paid Meta ads, rather than letting it disappear after one use
  • Personally shot and edited content — storyboarding, filming and editing my own TikTok videos, several of which reached hundreds of thousands of views

Email & CRM

Alongside influencer content, I rebuilt Ruby Cup's newsletters from the ground up — new templates, sharper subject lines, better segmentation. Where previous emails generated close to no direct sales, the rebuilt programme grew to consistently deliver a third of monthly revenue on its own.

Result

Combined, the influencer and CRM programmes went from a negligible contribution to a third of monthly revenue each — built from zero, with no prior playbook to work from.
