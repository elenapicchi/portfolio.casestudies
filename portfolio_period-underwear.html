+9,000% sales growth: repositioning a product no one was buying

The challenge

Period underwear was Ruby Cup's lowest-performing product. Customers weren't buying it, the messaging wasn't landing, and it was being treated as an afterthought in the content calendar. The brief was simple and daunting: turn it around.

This is a classic positioning problem — a product that works well but isn't being communicated in a way that makes sense to the audience it's meant for.

The approach

Audience research first. Before writing a word of copy, I mapped out who was actually buying (and not buying) period underwear, what their objections were, and what language they used to describe the problem the product solves. The insight: people weren't opposed to the product, they were confused about it — unsure how it worked, whether to trust it, and whether it was worth the price.

The repositioning wasn't about the product — it was about making the customer feel informed and confident enough to try something new. The copy shifted from product features to personal outcomes.

Content campaign built around education + proof. I developed a multi-channel content strategy that led with education (how does period underwear actually work?) and followed with social proof, sustainability credentials and a clear price-value argument — particularly relevant given the 2024 VAT exemption on period pants, which I turned into a timely news piece that drove organic traffic.

Channel-specific execution. Each channel had a distinct job: Instagram for visual storytelling and reach, TikTok for raw product education, email for nurturing and converting warm audiences, and paid Meta campaigns for acquisition. I wrote and produced content for all four, with A/B testing on landing pages and ad creative throughout.

The result

Sales grew by over 9,000% on the period underwear category. The product went from worst performer to a genuine growth driver. The overall conversion rate improvement of +92% came from the combination of clearer landing page copy, stronger product positioning and a better-structured email flow for people who'd shown interest but hadn't yet converted.

Why this is relevant to B2B investment content

The core skill here is identical to what investment content requires: taking a topic that's confusing or intimidating to the target audience, and making it feel accessible, credible and worth acting on. Whether that's period underwear or patient capital, the formula is the same — understand the audience's hesitation, address it directly, and give them a reason to believe.
